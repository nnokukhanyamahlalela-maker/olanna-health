2️⃣ Lotus-Based Animation System (App Logic)
🌸 Core Concept

The lotus is not decorative — it is data-driven.

How it works:

Cycle phase determines lotus state

Symptoms affect petal behaviour

Mood + energy subtly alter colour saturation

Examples:

Severe cramps → soft terracotta glow at base

High energy → petals open slightly wider

Low mood → colours desaturate gently

Ovulation → soft gold halo appears briefly

⚠️ Never spin, bounce, or sparkle.
This is calm embodiment, not gamification.

Where the Lotus Appears

Home screen (primary anchor)

Daily check-in header

Cycle insights screen

Subtle watermark in charts
