Guiding Principles

Olanna Health is a wellness tracking and educational application.

The system must:

Allow users to log health-related information

Provide reminders and educational prompts

Generate simple summaries and charts

Never provide medical diagnoses or treatment recommendations

All insights must be descriptive summaries of user-logged data, not medical interpretations.

Example acceptable insight:

"You logged cramps 4 times this cycle."

Example unacceptable insight:

"You may have endometriosis."

Global Database Structure

    -- sexual_health

- entry_time (time, nullable)

- cycle_phase (text, nullable)
    -- menstrual
    -- follicular

- notes (text, nullable)

- updated_at (timestamp)

This allows all trackers to share one logging structure.

Safety Language

All UI language must avoid diagnosis.

Allowed language:

log

reflect

track

notice patterns

stay informed

educational information

routine reminder

Avoid:

diagnose

detect conditions

medical risk assessment

treatment recommendations

App Disclaimer

This disclaimer should appear in onboarding and settings.

Olanna Health Disclaimer

Olanna Health is a wellness tracking and educational tool designed to support personal awareness of menstrual and sexual health. The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Any summaries, reminders, or insights are based solely on information you choose to log and are intended for personal awareness only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
